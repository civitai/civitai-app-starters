Chat replies use the viewer's free daily allowance (`X-Civitai-Tier: free`) while it lasts. When it runs out, the chat pays only if the viewer allowed it in Settings ("When they run out, continue on Buzz"); otherwise the reply offers Continue with Buzz. A viewer who allows paying also gets a paid reply when a free one has not started after 12 seconds. Settings shows the free replies left and can turn the allowance off.
