`<civitai-chat>` keeps recovering from network drops through a long reply. The retries a reply the browser's network cut off gets are now counted per drop, not per reply: a drop gets up to three retries, and the count starts again once a step finishes after a retry, so a reply of many minutes lives through several network changes; one reply still retries at most 10 times in all, so a network that keeps dropping ends it. The waits are now 1.5 s, 4 s and 8 s (were 500 ms and 2 s): a browser drops its connections twice about a second apart on one network change, and a retry inside that second failed at once and used up a try. `reply-retry`'s `attempt` counts the reply's retries. A drop that ends a reply after an earlier error the reply got past by itself (it carried on and finished more tool steps) is now retried: the cut is the error that ended the reply, so tools of steps that finished before it no longer block the retry, while a tool call in the cut step itself, or after it, still does. The error trace (`reply-error`'s `trace`, `assistant.errorTrace`) now describes that ending error throughout: its `stage`, `step` and `lastTool` were taken from the first error while its `message` and `name` came from the last.
