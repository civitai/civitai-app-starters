Models Civitai routes to other providers are premium models, off by default: the message box shows no model button and `/model` is not offered, and every chat answers with the default model, also one saved with another. Settings → **Premium models** turns them on. Their prices show in Buzz and as an estimate ("~195 in · ~793 out Buzz/M tokens", at 1,300 Buzz per USD of the provider's cost), since a reply is charged what it actually cost.
