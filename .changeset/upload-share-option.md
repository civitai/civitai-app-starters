**Share a viewer-picked upload with your app's other viewers.** `useImageUpload({ share: true })` (display uploads, blocking or `asyncScan`) sends `share: true` on `OPEN_IMAGE_UPLOAD`. The host tells the viewer in its upload dialog, before they pick a file, that the image will be visible to the app's other viewers once Civitai has scanned it. The server then records your app, from the verified block token, on the image. Your app's gated image read (`useGatedImages`) returns it to other signed-in viewers once it is scanned. Shared uploads are held to SFW (PG / PG13) and clamped to each viewer's own browsing level. `open()` resolves with `shared: boolean` (new `BlockSharedUploadImageInfo` / `BlockSharedPendingImageInfo` types, and an optional `shared` on `IMAGE_UPLOAD_RESULT`): `true` only when the host reports the server recorded the share, `false` otherwise, including on a host that predates sharing. Without `share` (the default) nothing changes: the upload stays private to the uploader, and the message is byte-identical to before. `share` is ignored for `generationSource` and `trainingData`. The host's no-token refusal (`no block token`) of a shared upload makes `open()` reject with the host's error rather than resolve `null`; its review-mode and pre-handshake refusals reply as a dismiss, so `open()` resolves `null` there. `createMockHost` gains `cannedImageUploadShared` (default `true`) and `imageUploadShareRefused` to exercise the shared, not-shared and refused replies, and `createLiveHost` answers a shared upload with the host's dismiss shape, `shared: false`. A host that predates the field ignores it, so the upload is not shared there. The pinned `./blocks` export count moves from 242 to 244.
